FAQ

The questions you're already asking.

What does 100% commission actually mean here?

You keep your full commission on every closing and pay one flat fee per transaction. No splits, no franchise fees, no royalty math. Ask Heidy for the exact fee in your confidential chat — it's one number, not a rate sheet.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term lock-in. If The Olea Group stops earning your business, you can leave — and we'd rather keep you with support than with a contract.

How does switching brokerages work?

In Florida it's a license transfer, not a career event. We handle the paperwork with you, your pending deals are worked out case by case, and most agents are fully moved in days — quietly.

Do I get leads?

Honest answer: sometimes. Company and builder leads are assigned situationally — mostly earned by production and responsiveness. They're a perk here, not a promise. No brokerage can guarantee lead flow, including this one.

Do you really serve Spanish-speaking clients?

Yes — the brokerage operates in English and Spanish daily, from first call to closing table. If you're bilingual, your skill finally has a brokerage built around it. If you're not, the in-house team backs you up.
